How to play

Cover every square with the dominoes from the tray. Every colored region has a rule that must hold when completed. Dots are pip values (a blank half is zero).

Tap a domino and then its starting square. Use Rotate for vertical placement, Flip to swap halves, and tap a placed domino to remove it. Undo reverses a move.

Green border: completed region meets its rule. Red border: the rule is broken or already impossible.

Keyboard: R rotate, F flip, Z undo, N new puzzle.
