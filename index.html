How to play

Cover every square with the dominoes from the tray. Every outlined region has a rule that must hold when completed. Plain gray squares accept any pip value. Dots are pip values (a blank half is zero).

Drag a domino from the tray toward the board. The dotted outlines mark each clue region. The full domino magnetically previews the nearest available two-square position; release to place it. Drag placed dominoes to move them, or drag them off the board to return them to the tray. Tap any domino to rotate it through four orientations, including reversing the ends. You can also select a tray domino and tap an empty square to place it.

Green border: completed region meets its rule. Red border: the rule is broken or already impossible.
