How to play

Cover every square with the dominoes from the tray. Every colored region has a rule that must hold when completed. Dots are pip values (a blank half is zero).

Drag a full domino from the tray and hover over the board to preview its snapped location; release to place. Drag placed dominoes to move them, or drag them off the board to return them to the tray. Tap any domino to rotate it through four orientations, including reversing the ends. You can also select a tray domino and tap an empty square to place it.

Green border: completed region meets its rule. Red border: the rule is broken or already impossible.

Keyboard: Z undo, N new puzzle.
